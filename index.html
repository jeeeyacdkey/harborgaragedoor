Safety first

Do not fix springs or cables yourself

Garage door springs and lift cables hold a lot of stored tension. A release can cause serious injury. Until a contractor arrives:

  • Do not stand under or walk through a door that is partly open.
  • Do not try to force the door up or down.
  • Keep children and pets away from the door.
  • Pull the red emergency release only if the door is fully closed or fully open.

Questions

Before you request

Do you repair garage doors?

No. We are a referral service only. We connect you with an independent local contractor, and the contractor performs and is responsible for the work.

Does it cost anything to use this service?

Sending a request does not cost you anything. The contractor sets the price for repairs and quotes you directly. We may receive a referral fee from the contractor.

Who sets the price and the warranty?

The contractor does. Ask for a written quote and the warranty terms on parts and labor before you agree to any work.

What should I ask the contractor?

Ask for proof of insurance, their Massachusetts registration or license where it applies, the total price including parts, and when they can arrive.

Is there a guaranteed arrival time?

No. Timing depends on the contractor's schedule and your location. Mark your request as urgent if the door is stuck open or will not close.
